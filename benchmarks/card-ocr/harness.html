What this experiment measures

Automatic text discovery, title recognition, catalog matching and spatial deduplication. The additional edge detector currently targets orange sleeves. It does not infer hidden card identities or claim an exact deck count. Evaluation labels are loaded only by the separate scoring script.

The first run downloads OCR models and runtime assets. Your selected photograph is never uploaded. For reproducible measured results, see PHOTO-RESULTS.md in this benchmark directory.
